He was a one of instructors in an ordinary ESL school.

He was a boring teacher.

Everyone yawned when he tried to teach something even himself.

I remember that my Japanese friend told me about him.

"He's better to find another job."

"You think so? like what? I think he just feels boring with his life."

"I think so. I think he should be a Tim Hortons guy."

"That's funny. It's really suitable job for him. How can you think of it?!"

I still remember his yawning face.

I still remember his name.

I still remember him.
